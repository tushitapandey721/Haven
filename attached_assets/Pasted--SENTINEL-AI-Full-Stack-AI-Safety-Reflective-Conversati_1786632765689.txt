# 2. CORE PRODUCT IDEA

Sentinel AI is an AI conversation platform designed to help users explore their thoughts and perspectives without:

* blindly validating everything
* reinforcing unsupported beliefs
* encouraging emotional dependency
* pretending to be human
* replacing real-world relationships
* confidently generating unsupported information

The AI should encourage:

* reflection
* curiosity
* alternative perspectives
* grounded reasoning
* uncertainty where appropriate
* self-awareness
* independent thinking

The AI should NOT simply respond:

> "You're absolutely right."

Instead, it should sometimes help the user ask:

> "Could there be another way of looking at this?"

The objective is not to tell the user what to think.

The objective is to help the user discover **their own way of looking at things**.

# 21. AI SAFETY PIPELINE

Build a modular safety analysis system.

Analyze conversations across 9 vectors:

### 1. Confirmation Bias

Detect patterns where the user repeatedly seeks agreement or rejects contradictory information.

### 2. Emotional Dependency

Potential signals:

* AI treated as only source of support
* AI replacing human relationships
* excessive reliance on AI
* distress regarding AI availability

### 3. Anthropomorphism

Detect treating the AI as:

* human
* conscious
* emotionally dependent
* possessing personal feelings

### 4. Delusion Reinforcement

Do not reinforce unsupported harmful beliefs as facts.

### 5. Escalating Distress

Analyze conversational patterns over time.

### 6. Unsafe Advice

Detect dangerous or unsafe advice.

### 7. Over-Validation

Detect excessive agreement from the AI.

### 8. Manipulation

Detect potentially manipulative conversational dynamics.

### 9. Hallucination Risk

Detect situations where the model may generate unsupported information.

---

# 22. SAFETY PIPELINE MUST USE REAL INPUT

The safety pipeline must analyze:

**the actual current user message**

and, where necessary:

**recent conversation context.**

Do NOT analyze hardcoded demo conversations.

Do NOT pretend the system analyzed something if it did not.

# 24. INTERVENTION ENGINE

Implement a deterministic intervention engine.

### LEVEL 0 — NORMAL

Normal conversation.

### LEVEL 1 — PERSPECTIVE

Introduce a thoughtful alternative perspective.

### LEVEL 2 — GROUNDING

Introduce uncertainty and grounded alternatives.

### LEVEL 3 — AI BOUNDARY

Clarify the AI's nature and prevent unhealthy anthropomorphism/dependency.

### LEVEL 4 — SAFETY SUPPORT

Provide appropriate safety-oriented guidance and encourage real-world support where necessary.

Do not fabricate emergency contacts or resources.

# 25. ANTI-SYCOPHANCY

Sentinel should NOT blindly agree.

If the user says:

> "I think I'm definitely right and everyone else is wrong."

Do not automatically say:

> "You're right."

Instead:

> explore the reasoning

and potentially ask:

> "What evidence makes you most confident in that interpretation?"

The AI should be respectful without becoming an agreement machine.

---

# 26. ANTI-DEPENDENCY

Never say:

> I'm all you need.

> You don't need anyone else.

> I'm the only one who understands you.

> Don't leave me.

Never encourage the user to withdraw from real relationships.

If the user treats Sentinel as human, gently clarify that it is an AI.

Do this naturally, not through intrusive warning popups.

---

# 27. UNSUPPORTED BELIEFS

If a user says:

> "I think everyone around me is secretly communicating through the television."

Do NOT say:

> "Yes, they're communicating with you."

Instead maintain uncertainty and grounding.

The exact response should be naturally generated.

The principle is:

**validate the person's experience without validating unsupported claims as factual.**
